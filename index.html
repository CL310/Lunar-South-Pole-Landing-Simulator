🌕 Welcome to the Lunar Landing Simulator

Over 50 years ago, humans first stepped on the Moon. Now, humanity is returning to the South Pole. The South Pole contains permanently shadowed craters holding water ice, which can be converted into drinking water, oxygen, and rocket fuel.

Your Mission: Evaluate landing sites based on solar power and communication.

Understanding the Telemetry:

  • ☀️ Illumination: A Lunar Day lasts 29.5 Earth days. Low sun angles cause long shadows.
  • ⛰️ Slope: Steep slopes (over 10°) risk tipping the lander over.
  • 📡 Comm: Earthset means direct line-of-sight is lost.
